The Missing Painting

A story game about art, memories, and courage.

Story Introduction

You are a young artist preparing for a city art exhibition. The night before the exhibition, your finished painting disappears. You fall into a dream made from old memories.

Explore six locations, collect two important items, and learn why the painting disappeared.
